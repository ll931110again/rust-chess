Goal

Curated news is a project to replace our web browsing habits.

Look into our browsing, most of the websites are sensational, low quality, and click bait. These web pages are just there to damage our mental capacity.

Instead of trying to carefully manage the list of articles / news (and still fail anyways), we will be curating our own news feed, ensuring these are only high quality news, articles, videos, and so forth.

Current news also focus on many popular topics (politics, Trump, etc.), leaving a vast majority of topics unexplored and unnoticed.

----

Design

There will be two prongs approach: high quality human level news, and synthesized news by AI.

On first approach, we will be systematically crawl the web to continuously build a database of articles, and have GPT assign a score for the articles, based on quality, non sensational and informative.

On second approach, we will be taking current news, but rewrite them to be nonsensational, high quality and informative. You can pull in the information from multiple news sources to amend the writing.

Note that we will be expecting the crawler to continuously build and indexing the web, to ensure our news feed stay consistently fresh.

---

To sum up:
* We expect high quality articles.
* We expect articles of topics that might not be mainstream.
